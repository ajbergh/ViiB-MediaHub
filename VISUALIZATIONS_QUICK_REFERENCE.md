## Tips for Best Results

1. **Match to mood**: Choose visualizations that complement the emotional tone of your music
2. **Experiment**: Try different visualizations with the same song to find your favorite
3. **Genre pairing**: Use the genre guide above for recommendations
4. **Full-screen**: Visualizations look best in Now Playing full-screen mode
5. **Lighting**: Many glow effects shine best in darker environments
6. **High-quality audio**: Better audio = more responsive, beautiful visualizations

## Keyboard Shortcuts (When in Now Playing)

- **Space**: Play/Pause
- **Q/E**: Previous/Next track
- **Escape**: Exit Now Playing

Change visualizer in Settings → Audio → Visualizer Style
